Our Heritage & Mission

A Tradition of Excellence Since 1964

Founded on the belief that education should nurture both intellect and character, St. Margret School offers a balanced, forward-thinking curriculum rooted in timeless values. Our dedicated faculty fosters an inclusive environment where critical thinking, creativity, and service to others flourish.

Whether in our state-of-the-art STEM innovation labs, fine arts studios, or championship athletic fields, St. Margret students learn to collaborate, persevere, and lead with empathy.
